About Us

Innovative Concepts is a dedicated 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization established in 2008, committed to enhancing the quality of life for disabled adults in the District of Columbia. We proudly partner with the Department on Disability Services (DDS) to provide community based housing, eliminating employment barriers rn hance employability skills, and provide person centered planning and develop neccesary supports to maintain individual career and personal goals.
