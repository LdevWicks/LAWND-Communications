Welcome to Charlotte's Web

Charlotte's Web Enrichment Center, Inc. is a 501(c)3 non-profit organization. We are established to aid in the care for seniors, provide an outreach, structure and growth to the youth through our boxing and mentoring program.


We believe that our community is responsible for providing the necessary tools to enhance the lives of the youth and seniors. Through our programs, we will prepare our youth to be productive and positive young adults and we are committed to further improve the quality of life to our seniors.
