About Us

Innovative Concepts is a dedicated 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization established in 2008, committed to enhancing the quality of life for disabled adults in the District of Columbia. We proudly partner with the Department on Disability Services (DDS) to provide vital residential support services, ensuring that our community members receive the care and assistance they need to lead fulfilling and independent lives.
